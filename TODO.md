# Mike's PS5 To-Do List

No printer needed. Do the jobs in order. Write your answers in the empty spaces.

Remember: 1 cm = 10 mm.

**Words we use**
- **Front:** the side with the disc slot and the USB port.
- **Back:** the side with the HDMI port.
- **Drive end:** the end where the disc drive is.
- **Fan end:** the other end.

---

## Job 1: How fat is the PS5?

We have two answers and only one can be right: 104 mm or 114 mm. Let's find out!

**You need:** tape measure, the biggest hard book you own, a sheet of paper, and a flat table.

- [ ] Turn the PS5 all the way off (not rest mode).
- [ ] Take out the disc and pull out **every** cable.
- [ ] Wait 10 minutes so it can cool down.
- [ ] Take off the stand.
- [ ] Put the paper on the table so the PS5 doesn't get scratched.
- [ ] Lay the PS5 down with the **disc bump facing up**. It may wobble a little. That's okay. Don't push on it.
- [ ] Lay the book on top and keep it as flat as you can.
- [ ] Slide the book around slowly to find where it sits highest.
- [ ] Measure from the table up to the **bottom of the book**. Do this in 3 places.
- [ ] If the book is tipped, measure its low side and its high side, then use the number in the middle.
- [ ] Write down the biggest number.

**Disc bump up:** 96 mm

- [ ] Flip the PS5 over gently and do it all again.

**Disc bump down:** 95 mm

**What does it mean?**
- Both numbers near 104: the computer model is too fat, and Claude will fix it.
- Both numbers near 114: the model is right. Hooray!
- Something else, or two very different numbers: tell Claude the numbers anyway.

## Job 1C: How far does the plate slide? (needed now)

The new plates overhang both ends by exactly this distance, so they can slide off the way the stock ones do. Nobody has published this number, so only your tape can answer it. Do it **3 times on each plate** (top and bottom), and write down the biggest number.

**You need:** tape measure, a small piece of sticky tape, and a pen.

- [ ] Turn the PS5 off and pull out every cable.
- [ ] Lay the PS5 down with the **disc bump facing down**, so the flat white plate is on top.
- [ ] Stick the tape across the edge, half on the white plate and half on the black part.
- [ ] Draw one straight line across the tape, from the white part onto the black part.
- [ ] Slice the tape along the edge between white and black, so the plate can move.
- [ ] Slide the plate off the way you do when you clean the PS5, but **stop as soon as it comes loose**.
- [ ] Measure how far apart the two halves of your line are now.

**Slide distance:** ______ mm

- [ ] While the plate is off: measure how long one of the plate's hooks is, and how long the slot it goes into on the black part is.

**Hook length:** ______ mm   **Slot length:** ______ mm

- [ ] Put the plate back until it clicks, and peel off the tape.

## Job 1D: Where are the plugs and buttons?

The new plates have edges that cover part of the black middle. Claude needs to know where every plug and button is, so the edges get holes in the right places.

**Update 2026-11-09:** Claude read most of this from an X-ray of the PS5, so the big table is gone. All the plugs sit inside the black stripe. Some plug heads poke a little past the old stripe on the **bottom** side, so the stripe was made wider on that side (no notches). One measurement checks that the plugs clear it.

From the X-ray (±4 mm, measured from the drive end): Power 30–44, HDMI 71–85, LAN 106–123, USB 131–145 and 155–169. On the front, USB-C is at 166–176 and USB-A at 183–197. The front buttons sit inside the stripe, so they don't need holes. The black part at the back is already measured: 80 mm at the drive end and 45 mm at the fan end.

**You need:** tape measure and a flat table.

- [ ] Turn the PS5 all the way off and pull out every cable.
- [ ] Lay the PS5 down with the **disc bump facing down**, the way it will sit with the new plates.
- [ ] At the **back**, find the HDMI port. Measure from the **bottom edge of the HDMI port** down to the **bottom edge of the black part**, where the black meets the white plate.

**HDMI bottom edge to black bottom edge:** ______ mm

- [ ] If you like: plug in the HDMI cable and check whether its plug head sticks out past the black onto the white plate. **Plug head on the white?** yes / no

## Job 2: Make a pretend plate (skipped, see job 3)

A cardboard plate shows you how the real one will look, before anyone prints anything.

**You need:** tape measure, a big piece of cardboard, scissors, a pen, sticky tape, books, and a phone for photos.

### Make the top
- [ ] Cut a cardboard rectangle **50 cm long** and **25.8 cm wide**.
- [ ] The extra length is for handles, so draw a line **5.8 cm** from each short end. The part between the lines is your pretend plate.

### Set it up
- [ ] Lay the PS5 on the paper with the **disc bump facing down**.
- [ ] Take off the top plate, the same way as when you clean the PS5. Keep it somewhere safe.
- [ ] Don't touch the fan.
- [ ] Make a stack of books at each short end of the PS5. Both stacks should be as tall as your **Disc bump down** number from Job 1.
- [ ] Use paper to make the stacks a little taller if you need to.
- [ ] Rest the cardboard handles on the stacks, so the pretend plate floats over the PS5 without pressing on it.

### Try edge A: the slanted edge
- [ ] Cut a long cardboard strip, about 39 cm long and 5 cm tall.
- [ ] Hold it against the front edge of the pretend plate.
- [ ] At each end, make a dot on the strip **4 mm above** where the black part starts.
- [ ] Join the dots with a straight line.
- [ ] Cut along the line and tape the strip to the pretend plate.
- [ ] Make another one the same way for the back.
- [ ] Step back, crouch down until your eyes are level with the PS5, and look.
- [ ] Take one photo of the front and one of the back.

### Try edge B: the level edge
- [ ] Take off the slanted strips.
- [ ] Cut new strips with a bottom edge that stays the same height all the way along.
- [ ] Find the spot where the black part comes up closest to the plate, and leave a **4 mm** gap there. The gap gets bigger everywhere else.
- [ ] Tape them on, then take the same two photos.

### Tidy up
- [ ] Put the real top plate back on until it clicks.
- [ ] Put the stand back on.
